FAQs

Questions before you pack?

Can’t find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp.

Are meals included in the price?

Yes. Unlimited breakfast, plus veg & non-veg lunch and dinner buffets are included with every stay.

Do water activities cost extra?

No. Boating, kayaking, zorbing, rafting and river swimming are free in the package.

Is transportation included?

Transportation is charged extra. Let us know your pickup point and we’ll share the cost.

Can I bring my pet?

Yes, pets are allowed at Dandelifun.

Can we host a birthday or team event?

Yes. A stage is provided for special events. Starters are available at an extra charge.

How is the price calculated?

Prices are per person, per night: Deluxe ₹1800, Standard ₹1600, Commando Tent ₹1300, Camping ₹1000.
